# Research Gap Investigation

## Working Area

Reliability and visual evidence sensitivity of Vision-Language Models
for autonomous driving.

## Current Status

The broad problem is established in the literature.

We must NOT claim novelty for:
- generic driving VLM evaluation
- degradation robustness alone
- hallucination under missing visual input alone
- counterfactual reasoning alone
- temporal consistency alone

These areas have existing recent literature.

## Objective

Identify a narrower experimentally testable question that remains
insufficiently investigated after systematic prior-art comparison.

## Novelty Kill Test

A proposed gap will be rejected if a prior paper has already:
1. studied the same driving task,
2. used the same type of intervention,
3. measured substantially the same phenomenon,
4. and drawn essentially the same conclusion.

## Candidate Direction

Evidence sensitivity under controlled visual interventions.
